# Capstone Project Brief - reference example

Sample reference written by the REX AI team (house style for capstone briefs).

## Style rules
- Start with a realistic scenario from the learners' industry: who the client is and what problem they have.
- Requirements say what the solution must do, not how.
- Deliverables: project plan, working product or analysis, short report, final presentation.
- Milestones are dated by course week; the instructor reviews the plan before building starts.
- The brief states the rules on teamwork, plagiarism and the use of AI tools.

## Example scenario
A private hospital group wants to reduce patient waiting times in its outpatient clinics. You are part of the
quality team and must analyse three months of appointment data, find the main causes of delays and present a
dashboard and three recommendations to the operations director.
