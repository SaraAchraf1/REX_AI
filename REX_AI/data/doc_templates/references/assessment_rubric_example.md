# Assessment Rubric - reference example

Sample reference written by the REX AI team (house style for analytic rubrics).

## Style rules
- Four performance levels: Excellent (4), Good (3), Satisfactory (2), Needs improvement (1).
- Three to five criteria per assessment; the points of the criteria add up to the assessment total.
- Descriptors are observable ("dashboard answers the business question with correct figures"), not vague
  ("good work").
- Share the rubric with learners before the assessment.

## Example criterion
Technical accuracy (10 points)
- Excellent: all calculations and figures are correct and verified against the source data.
- Good: minor errors that do not change the conclusions.
- Satisfactory: several errors; conclusions partly affected.
- Needs improvement: major errors; conclusions not supported.
