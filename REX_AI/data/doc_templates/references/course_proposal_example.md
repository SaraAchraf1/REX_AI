# Training Proposal - reference example

Sample reference written by the REX AI team (house style for proposals to client organisations and tenders).

## Style rules
- Open with an executive summary of 3-4 sentences: the client's need, the proposed programme, the result.
- Restate the client's objectives in their words, then map each objective to modules.
- Methodology: state the share of hands-on practice and how progress is measured.
- Investment uses only the catalogue fee and valid promotions; group or tender pricing is "to be confirmed".
- Quality section: show framework alignment as coverage, with the disclaimer that alignment is not accreditation.

## Example executive summary
The Ministry's digital transformation unit needs analysts who can turn operational data into decisions. We propose a
48-hour blended programme in data analysis, delivered over six weeks, in which every participant builds a dashboard
on the unit's own indicators. Progress is measured through module quizzes, practical labs and a capstone project.

## Example next steps
1. Confirm the number of participants and preferred dates.
2. Agree the capstone datasets with the client's data owner.
3. Sign the training agreement and receive the joining instructions.
