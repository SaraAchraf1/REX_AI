# Presentation Outline - reference example

Sample reference written by the REX AI team (house style for training slides).

## Style rules
- One idea per slide; at most five bullets of at most eight words each.
- Every slide has speaker notes: what to say, an example, and a question to ask the room.
- Each module starts with a section slide showing the module objectives and ends with an activity slide.
- Use the academy colours; no more than one chart or image per slide.

## Example slide
Slide 7: Why data cleaning matters
- Most analysis time is spent preparing data
- Wrong values produce wrong decisions
- Cleaning steps must be documented

Speaker notes: Share an example of a report that was wrong because of duplicate records. Ask: "What is the most
common data problem in your team?"
