# Facilitator Guide - reference example

Sample reference written by the REX AI team (house style for instructor / facilitator guides).

## Style rules
- Written for the instructor: imperative voice ("Ask learners to...", "Demonstrate...").
- Every module has objectives, a timed plan, activity instructions, check-for-understanding questions and common
  difficulties.
- Timing follows the academy pattern: 10% opening and recap, 35% instruction and demonstration, 40% guided
  practice, 15% review and check for understanding.
- The preparation checklist lists rooms or virtual labs, software, datasets and printed material.

## Example timed plan (3-hour session)
- 0:00-0:20  Recap of the previous module, objectives of today
- 0:20-1:20  Demonstration: cleaning a dataset step by step
- 1:20-2:30  Guided lab in pairs; circulate and check progress
- 2:30-3:00  Quick quiz, answer questions, preview next module

## Example common difficulties
- Learners delete rows with missing values without checking how many: ask them to count first.
