## Background

Delta Investment Bank is strengthening its security operations. The bank needs a training partner to raise
security awareness across all staff and to build the skills of its Security Operations Center (SOC) team.

## Scope of Work

Scope: Deliver (1) a 3-hour cybersecurity awareness course for 900 employees, online live, in Arabic and English;
and (2) a 60-hour SOC analyst program for 25 security staff covering network security, log analysis, SIEM,
incident response and threat hunting, with hands-on labs. Training takes place in Cairo.

## Eligibility Requirements

Eligibility:
- Egyptian company with a valid commercial register and tax card
- Minimum 5 years of experience delivering cybersecurity training
- ISO 9001 quality certification
