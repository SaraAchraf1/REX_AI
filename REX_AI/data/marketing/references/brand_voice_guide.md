# Andalusia Academy — Brand Voice and Claims Guide (SAMPLE)

Written by the team for the prototype. Replace with the academy's approved brand guide.

## Voice

- Helpful, confident and specific. We sell skills people can use, not hype.
- Lead with what the learner will be able to DO after the course, then the proof (hours, projects, instructor experience), then the offer.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Active voice ("You will build...", not "A dashboard will be built...").
- Address the reader as "you". In Arabic use Modern Standard Arabic that reads naturally to Egyptian and Saudi readers; avoid heavy dialect in ads.
- Emojis: at most two per post or message, never in e-mail subject lines or LinkedIn headlines.

## Facts and numbers

- Every fee, discount, date, number of hours, number of seats and instructor detail must come from the academy's approved data. Never round a price or invent a deadline.
- Quote the discounted price together with the promotion's condition and the date it ends.
- If a fact is missing (no schedule yet, no price), say "dates to be announced" or invite the reader to ask — never guess.
- Course content claims must match the approved curriculum or catalogue description.

## Claims we never make

- Never say a course is "accredited", "certified by", "endorsed by" or "officially approved" by any body. We may say the content is aligned with a framework (for example "prepares you for the PMP exam" when the catalogue says so).
- Never promise a job, a salary or a pass rate ("guaranteed job", "100% placement", "guaranteed pass").
- No comparisons with named competitors and no "best academy in Egypt" superlatives.
- Certificates: say "academy certificate of completion" unless the catalogue states otherwise.

## Calls to action

- One clear call to action per piece. Verbs first: "Register now", "Save your seat", "Ask us on WhatsApp".
- Pair urgency only with a real fact: seats left, the promotion end date or the cohort start date.

## Compliance

- Marketing e-mails and WhatsApp messages go only to people who gave consent; every message has an opt-out line.
- All campaign content is reviewed and approved by an academy administrator before it is published or sent.
