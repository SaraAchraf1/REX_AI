# Channel Playbook (SAMPLE)

Team-written guidance for each channel the Marketing Agent writes for. Replace with the academy's own playbook.

## Social media caption

- First line is the hook: a question the audience recognises or a concrete result. It must work before "see more".
- 3 to 5 short lines: the result, two proof points (hours, project, format), the call to action.
- 3 to 5 hashtags at the end: the course topic, the skill, the academy.
- Post when the audience is online: evenings in Egypt and Saudi Arabia (8–11 pm), weekends for students.

## Facebook / Instagram ad

- Headline up to 40 characters: the result or the offer.
- Primary text: the first 125 characters carry the message (the rest is hidden behind "more").
- Use one of Meta's call-to-action buttons: Learn More, Sign Up, Book Now, Contact Us, Apply Now, Get Offer.
- Test two angles against each other (for example outcome vs. offer) with the same audience and budget.
- Retarget people who visited the course page or engaged with the page in the last 30 days.

## LinkedIn post

- Professional tone; open with a work problem the reader has, then the skills the course gives.
- A short bulleted list of 3–4 learning outcomes performs well.
- Mention the instructor's experience if it is in the approved profile.
- 3–5 hashtags; no more than one emoji.
- Best for professionals, managers and HR / training departments (group and corporate offers).

## Marketing e-mail

- Subject line up to 60 characters, no emojis, no all caps. Preheader adds the main fact (start date or offer).
- Greeting, one-paragraph value statement, 3–4 outcome bullets, a details block (start date, format, schedule, fee and offer), one button-style call to action, signature.
- Send only to consented leads; include an unsubscribe line.
- Send the reminder 7–14 days before the cohort starts and a last call 2–3 days before.

## WhatsApp promotional message

- Only to contacts who opted in. Keep it under 450 characters: who it is for, the result, start date, price/offer, one call to action.
- End with how to stop messages ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe").
- A reply keyword (for example "Reply 1 to book a call") makes follow-up easy for the sales team.
