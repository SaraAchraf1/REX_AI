# Cybersecurity Fundamentals Reference Syllabus

Sample reference syllabus compiled by the REX AI team from public entry-level security curricula and
SOC analyst job postings in Egypt and Saudi Arabia.

## Module: Security concepts and the threat landscape
- Confidentiality, integrity and availability (CIA triad)
- Threat actors, attack vectors and the cyber kill chain
- Common attacks: phishing, malware, ransomware, social engineering
Outcome: Identify common threats and explain how they affect an organisation.

## Module: Networking and security fundamentals
- TCP/IP, ports and protocols
- Firewalls, VPNs and network segmentation
- Analysing traffic with Wireshark
Outcome: Analyse network traffic to spot suspicious activity.

## Module: Identity and access management
- Authentication, authorisation and multi-factor authentication
- Least privilege and role-based access control
- Password policies and privileged access
Outcome: Configure access controls following least privilege.

## Module: Security controls and hardening
- System hardening for Windows and Linux
- Patch and vulnerability management
- Cryptography basics: hashing, symmetric and asymmetric encryption
Outcome: Apply hardening controls to a server.

## Module: Security monitoring and incident response
- Logs, SIEM and alert triage
- The incident response lifecycle
- Writing an incident report
Outcome: Triage a security alert and document the incident.

## Module: Governance, risk and compliance
- Security policies and awareness programmes
- Risk assessment basics
- Standards and regulations: ISO 27001, NCA ECC (Saudi Arabia), Egypt's data protection law
Outcome: Assess the risk of a simple scenario and propose controls.
