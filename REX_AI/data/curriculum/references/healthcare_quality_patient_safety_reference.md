# Healthcare Quality and Patient Safety Reference Syllabus

Sample reference syllabus compiled by the REX AI team from public healthcare-quality course outlines.
The academy has no healthcare course yet; this reference supports new course design.

## Module: Healthcare systems and quality
- Healthcare systems in Egypt and Saudi Arabia
- Dimensions of healthcare quality
- Accreditation bodies and standards in healthcare (overview only)
Outcome: Describe the dimensions of quality in healthcare.

## Module: Patient safety
- Medical errors and adverse events
- Root cause analysis
- Safety culture and incident reporting
Outcome: Analyse an adverse event with root cause analysis.

## Module: Infection prevention and control
- Hand hygiene and standard precautions
- Infection surveillance basics
Outcome: Apply standard infection control precautions.

## Module: Quality improvement tools
- PDSA cycles
- Process mapping and fishbone diagrams
- Quality indicators and dashboards
Outcome: Plan a quality improvement project using the PDSA cycle.

## Module: Health information and documentation
- Electronic health records
- Clinical documentation standards
- Confidentiality and patient data protection
Outcome: Use health information systems while protecting patient confidentiality.
